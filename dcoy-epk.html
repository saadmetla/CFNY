Mississauga — Hip Hop / R&B

A sound that pushes Punjabi artists into new spaces.

— Harjot & Naji, Mississauga

DCOY is the Mississauga-based hip hop and R&B duo of Harjot and Naji. Sharp songwriting, strong production, and a natural chemistry between the two are creating a sound that feels rooted in Mississauga while pushing Punjabi artists into spaces they’ve rarely occupied.

There’s a confidence and ease to DCOY that sets the music apart — smooth melodies, sharp writing, and a distinct sense of personality drawn from their own experience and cultural identity. Rather than following the paths typically associated with Punjabi music, DCOY are making records that feel current, personal, and accessible to a wider audience.

Their music moves through relationships, ambition, nightlife, and the realities of growing up between cultures — personal without being confined to one audience. DCOY are building a sound that stays authentic to who they are while continuing to push forward creatively.
