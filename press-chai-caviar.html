I Know, I Suck. It's Been A Minute.

By Ihram Chowdhury · March 09, 2025 · Chai Caviar

Yeah, I've been MIA—crazy week on the business side of things, plus Ramadan (so I haven't been bumping music as much).

Happy Ramadan to everyone fasting!

BUT WE'RE BACK, and I got music updates for y'all. Let's get into it.

🎶 Girls Like You — "BEST FRIEND"

This is how you break an artist in 2025.

Girls Like You have been cooking up buzz through covers & social media, building an actual fanbase before even dropping a real song. And now, with "BEST FRIEND," they're capitalizing perfectly.

🔥 Why This Was a Masterclass in Artist Development:

  • The right team: Their management built their following first.
  • The right collaborators: Chani on the writing and track, Inderpal Moga on the feature—quality talent across the board.
  • The right execution: By the time they dropped their first major song, they already had real traction—and now, because the record is actually good, they can build on top of that momentum.

Big tip of the cap here. This is textbook artist marketing done right.

This record really remind me of an early 2000s R&B Record and I mean that with all the praise. I AM HERE FOR IT.

🎙️ Last Week's Episode: Saad Metla & The South Asian Music Industry

If you haven't tuned in yet, we dropped an episode with Saad Metla—founder of Capital Flight and the guy behind South Asian artist placements on On The Radar.

📢 Artists, if you have the budget, this is a PR hire worth considering.

One of the most interesting convos we had?

The struggle of conscious rap in Desi music.

Saad made a solid point—breaking a South Asian artist is already an uphill battle. But trying to break a Desi "Kendrick Lamar"? Even tougher.

Would love to hear y'all's thoughts after listening to the full convo.

💪 Gym Playlist + New Artist Plug

  • Saad Metla's Playlist — Been bumping this at the gym. Great curation, and I found some dope artists I hadn't heard before. Check it out
  • Random Plug: Saad also put me on to a Chicago artist named Babar. 🔥 Artists—hit him for a feature. Very early in his journey, but this kid has something.
