Chicago, IL — Rap / Hip-Hop

The next generation of Chicago rap — gritty, melodic, undeniable.

Babar is a rising rap artist hailing from Chicago — a city that has shaped his sound, his hustle, and his story. With a distinct voice and an unrelenting drive, Babar has been building a dedicated following both on the streets and online.

Revered in his community, Babar has caught the attention of major industry figures — most notably the late PNB Rock, whose recognition speaks to the authenticity and rawness of Babar’s artistry.

With nearly 1 million YouTube views and a growing digital footprint, Babar is poised to break through on a national scale. He represents the next generation of Chicago rap — gritty, melodic, and undeniable.
