Leicester, England · Hip Hop & Grime

One of the most compelling voices in UK hip hop and grime.

JJ Esko is a British rapper of Pakistani origin from Highfields, Leicester, England. Active since 2018, he has established himself as one of the most compelling and authentic voices in UK hip hop and grime — an artist whose music is inseparable from the life he has lived.

Esko released his debut single ‘Winning’ in December 2018, quickly catching the attention of GRM Daily, one of the UK’s leading urban music platforms. His follow-up, ‘Opp Block’, received a GRM Daily premiere and broke through, catching the ear of established UK rapper Geko, who personally reached out and invited Esko to join him on tour.

Drawing on personal experience — including periods of incarceration, loss, financial hardship, and street life in Leicester — Esko writes with a raw directness that resonates on a global scale. He has appeared on the BBC Asian Network (2019, 2022), Link Up TV, and GRM Daily, and has collaborated with internationally renowned artists including Imran Khan, Tegi Pannu, and Annural Khalid, cementing his reputation as a crossover force bridging UK rap and South Asian music.

Distributed through The Orchard, JJ Esko has amassed over 50 million streams, 27 million YouTube views, and a growing global fanbase across the UK, South Asia, and the wider diaspora. His album Yakeen debuted at #2 on the Spotify Top UK Albums chart, placing him directly alongside global heavyweights.

#2Yakeen — Spotify UK Albums Debut

Yakeen — #2 Spotify Top UK Albums Debut

JJ Esko’s album Yakeen made an immediate and powerful statement upon release, debuting at #2 on the Spotify Top UK Albums chart. The achievement placed him alongside the biggest names in British music, outperforming global acts and mainstream artists across all genres. ‘Yakeen’ — meaning ‘belief’ or ‘certainty’ in Urdu — is a statement of intent from an artist who has earned every bit of his platform through lived experience and relentless work.

WYNB — Single, 2020

Collaboration with Imran Khan

JJ Esko collaborated with Imran Khan — the global Bhangra and urban crossover star behind the worldwide smash ‘Amplifier’ — fusing UK rap with South Asian sound and significantly broadening Esko’s international reach.

Collaboration with Tegi Pannu

Esko linked up with one of the most in-demand voices in contemporary Punjabi music for a release that extended his crossover appeal across the UK and diaspora communities worldwide.

Mistakes — ft. Annural Khalid

JJ Esko collaborated with Pakistani singer-songwriter Annural Khalid on the track ‘Mistakes’. Annural is best known for her viral sensation ‘Jhol’, one of the biggest Pakistani music hits of recent years. The collaboration bridges Esko’s authentic UK street rap with the melodic richness of Pakistan’s mainstream music scene.
